Ana's Bedtime Tales: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 October 2026

Ana's Bedtime Tales is a picture-book app for young children and the grown-ups who read to them. We designed it so that it does not need any information about you or your child.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting and no third-party software development kits. The app does not request any device permissions, and it has no internet access at all.

Information kept on the device

The app keeps a few things in its own private storage on the device, so that it can work: your settings (such as story length and text size), the books it has made (the last ten, plus any you mark as favourites), and the page you were reading. This information never leaves the device. You can delete kept books at any time in the grown-ups area, and everything is removed when the app is uninstalled. The app excludes this data from cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.

Children

The app is designed for children aged 5 and under together with a parent or carer. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. Settings and the list of kept books are behind a simple parent check.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. A new version of the app will never start collecting personal information without updating this policy first.
